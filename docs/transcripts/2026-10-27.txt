Israel held a general election on October 27, 2026, the first since the Hamas attacks on October 7, 2023. More than seven million Israelis were eligible to vote, with early reports indicating a record turnout. The election is widely viewed as a referendum on Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu's leadership. Netanyahu, leading the right-wing Likud party, faces strong opposition from centrist and left-wing parties. The campaign focused heavily on security and defence, including the ongoing conflict with Hamas and Iran. Netanyahu accused opponents, including Arab Israeli parties, of attempting to steal the election and claimed arrests of party members with cash envelopes. The proportional representation system means coalitions will be needed to form a government.

Confirmed Ebola cases in the Democratic Republic of the Congo have exceeded 10,000, with 4,887 deaths reported. The outbreak is caused by the Bundibugyo virus, a rare Ebola strain without an approved vaccine or specific treatment. This is the fastest-growing and deadliest Ebola outbreak in the DRC's history. The virus has spread to Kenya, Nigeria, and Uganda, with Kenya reporting nine cases and two deaths. Clinical trials for vaccines and antiviral treatments, including the Ervebo vaccine approved for a different Ebola strain, are underway. Healthcare systems in affected areas face shortages of supplies and staff, attacks on health workers, and disruptions to routine services, contributing to increased maternal deaths and strikes over unpaid wages.

Federal prosecutors have charged former FBI Director James Comey with leaking classified national defence information to the New York Times in October 2016. This marks the third attempt by the US Justice Department to bring criminal charges against him. Comey denies any wrongdoing, describing the charges as an 'appalling abuse' of the Department of Justice. The charges were filed in Washington DC through a criminal information, bypassing grand jury approval. Comey's lawyer noted that two prior investigations during the Trump administration found no grounds for charges. Legal experts suggest the case may be dismissed due to the statute of limitations and see the charges as politically motivated. On the same day, a judge ordered the reinstatement of Comey's daughter, who was illegally fired by the Justice Department.

Jordan launched airstrikes against Islamic State militants in southern Syria on Monday night, in retaliation for a deadly attack on a funeral at a Greek Orthodox Church in Jordan. Jordanian officials claimed the strikes killed more than 20 militants and destroyed a large weapons depot. Syrian authorities reported that the strikes killed seven civilians, including three children, a woman, and two elderly men, contradicting Jordan's claim about militant casualties. Jordanian government spokesman Mohammed al-Momani expressed regret over civilian casualties resulting from the airstrikes. A Syrian resident, Masoud al-Salim, lost his wife, son, and three grandchildren in one of the strikes near the border village of Al-Tuff. Syria condemned the strikes as an unacceptable violation of its airspace and stated they were conducted without prior coordination with Damascus. Despite the incident, Jordan and Syria affirmed their intention to maintain strong bilateral ties and security coordination.

In the week before October 24, 2026, the French interior ministry contacted telecom operators about potentially blocking social networks or mobile internet in certain areas to hinder rioters' coordination. Prime Minister Sébastien Lecornu personally asked operators to prepare to block platforms including TikTok, Instagram, Snapchat, and X. This plan, linked to protests on October 24, was revealed by the French digital outlet Blast and reported by Politico Europe on October 27.

World Bank President Ajay Banga said on October 27, 2026, that capital availability remains strong despite rising interest rates linked to artificial intelligence. The World Bank mobilised $112 billion last year, triple the amount from three years earlier. Banga noted emerging markets focus AI investments on infrastructure like bridges and roads, avoiding investment hesitancy. Developed countries seek funding for large AI projects, differing from emerging markets. He acknowledged AI's transformative potential but warned of uncertainties ahead, ahead of global central bank rate decisions.

The UK Debt Management Office sold £4.5 billion of five-year government debt at an average yield of 5.036%, the highest since 2008. Demand was strong, with investors bidding 3.5 times the amount available, allowing the DMO to choose lower yields. This sale highlights high borrowing costs and limited fiscal flexibility ahead of the Chancellor's budget on 28 October 2026. Analysts expect a cautious budget focusing on fiscal control to ease pressure on gilt yields amid concerns over borrowing plans.

He apologized for the 'ill-judged' comment, stating he understands it hurt many people in China and Chinese communities worldwide. Several Chinese brands, including Mengniu Dairy and Dongpeng Beverage, terminated their endorsement contracts with Mbappé following the backlash. On October 27, 2026, 21 Indian news publishers challenged a government privacy law at the Supreme Court. The law mandates journalists to obtain consent from individuals they report on, which media groups say amounts to pre-censorship and threatens investigative journalism. The Digital News Publishers Association, representing major outlets like Network18 and NDTV, warns the law allows consent withdrawal and data erasure without public-interest safeguards, risking newsroom closures and press freedom.

In an October 2026 GQ interview, she denied speculation, confirming she sold one rented property. Aitana owns a Miami home for work, pays taxes in Spain, and supports higher taxes on the wealthy. A 30-year-old researcher named Youssef Siher was detained over clashes in Milan on 17 and 18 October 2026 and has refused to answer a judge's questions. On 24 October 2026, around 150 personal and home searches were conducted across several Italian provinces, resulting in the seizure of folding and butterfly knives, a hatchet engraved with '1312', knuckledusters, a telescopic baton, blank-firing pistols, glass breakers, balaclavas, and other offensive weapons. Siher is accused of incitement to commit crimes with terrorist intent and organized violence during public demonstrations and is alleged to run social media channels of the 17 October Movement. Coca-Cola has raised its full-year earnings per share forecast to at least 11%, up from 9 to 10%. The company expects organic revenue growth of at least 5%, slightly above its previous outlook.

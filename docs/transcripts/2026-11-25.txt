Huawei has been convicted in New York on multiple federal criminal charges, including fraud, U.S. sanctions violations, and conspiracy to steal trade secrets. A federal jury found Huawei and three of its subsidiaries guilty of several offenses, such as bank and wire fraud and conspiracy to commit money laundering. The trial, which lasted about three months, involved Huawei Technologies and its subsidiaries. These charges were first made public in 2019, shortly after the arrest of Huawei's Chief Financial Officer, Meng Wanzhou, in Canada. Sentencing for Huawei and its subsidiaries is scheduled for September 14, 2027.

Two Libyan soldiers have died from injuries sustained during a Ukrainian attack on Russian ships in Benghazi on 22 November 2026. The attack targeted the naval base of Benghazi and was described as a criminal attack by the eastern Libyan authorities. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky claimed responsibility for the attacks conducted near Benghazi on Sunday. According to Kiev, during the operation a corvette sank and a cargo ship was hit. Ukraine used seven domestically made maritime drones in the operation. The eastern Libyan authorities confirmed the deaths and condemned the attack as a treacherous Ukrainian aggression against the port of Benghazi. Ukraine's Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha stated that Ukraine respects Libya's sovereignty and that the attack aimed to counter Russian aggression, not to harm Libya or its people.

China launched the Shenzhou-24 spacecraft with a Pakistani astronaut on board, marking the first time a foreign national has flown to its Tiangong space station. The spacecraft lifted off at 8:18 am local time (0018 GMT) on 25 November 2026, a Wednesday morning, from the Jiuquan launch center in the Gobi Desert using a Long March-2F rocket. The crew includes Tang Hongbo, Wu Yi, and Pakistani pilot Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first foreigner to fly on a Chinese crewed mission. The China Manned Space Agency described the launch as a "complete success" after the capsule separated from the rocket about ten minutes later and entered orbit. During the mission, the crew will conduct more than 90 scientific experiments and technology tests. Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, aged 40, is on a short-term mission and will return to Earth aboard Shenzhou-23 after completing an experiment involving photosynthetic cyanobacteria.

Britain’s Defence Ministry and MI5 have issued a warning to companies supporting Ukraine’s military, cautioning they may face sabotage attempts from Russia. The Ministry of Defence and the National Protective Security Authority stated that organisations involved in military procurement or supplying equipment to Ukraine could be targeted. The sabotage is said to involve proxy actors using low-sophistication methods such as arson. Russia’s embassy in London denied these claims, asserting that Russia has no plans or intentions to attack the UK. Last year, three British men were convicted for an arson attack on a warehouse storing Starlink satellite equipment bound for Ukraine, an act prosecutors linked to Russian military intelligence. The Ministry of Defence is holding closed-door security briefings with critical infrastructure and defence leaders, with Security Minister Dan Jarvis highlighting the seriousness of recent sabotage attempts across Europe.

The Gambia held a presidential election on December 5, 2026, with eight candidates contesting, including incumbent President Adama Barrow, who sought a third term. The country faces significant challenges, including an overall unemployment rate of 8.3 percent in 2025, with youth unemployment at 11.5 percent among those aged 15 to 35. Businesses have been severely affected by frequent electricity outages, with power cuts lasting up to 19 hours or more between August and October 2026. Independent sources reported that 1,603 Gambians died attempting irregular migration in 2024, and 893 in 2025, most at sea. Additionally, a special tribunal to prosecute alleged crimes committed under former President Jammeh is expected to begin hearings early in 2027.

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang made a triumphant return to the semiconductor giant in an all-hands meeting Tuesday morning, apologizing to staff for his conduct during his previous stint at the company’s helm and vowing to do better this time around. The AI hardware leader resigned from the company on Nov. 12 amid mounting scrutiny about alleged romantic relationships with subordinates and Alice Huang, a former executive at Singaporean cloud computing company Megaspeed. Less than a week later, Nvidia’s board announced that Huang would return to the company — backed by input from senior levels of the United States government that essentially guaranteed Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk. Huang reportedly apologized to Nvidia employees on Tuesday, the day after returning to the company as chief executive, saying he made terrible mistakes and is now in therapy for 90 minutes per day. The company's board reinstated Huang on Monday in a shock U-turn. Harvey Jones, chairman of Nvidia's board, said Huang's return was in the best interests of stockholders, employees, partners, and customers. On Monday, former President Donald Trump said he spoke to the company's directors and convinced them to bring back Huang.

Senegal has been officially declared the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations champion following a ruling by the Court of Arbitration for Sport. However, the Moroccan Football Federation has announced it will challenge the verdict. Morocco is contesting the CAS decision, citing irregularities in the proceedings. The dispute centres on the awarding of the 2025 AFCON title, with both nations seeking recognition as champions.

Australia's inflation rate rose to 4.9% in the year to October, up from 4.3%, according to the Australian Bureau of Statistics. The increase was driven by surging fuel costs, which climbed 6.3% in October and are about 33% higher over the year, and the end of electricity rebates, with prices 25% higher than a year ago. Building costs inflation also accelerated to 5.1%, while childcare, hairdressing, and insurance prices all rose by more than 5%. The Reserve Bank's preferred trimmed mean inflation measure increased to 3.8%, exceeding expectations. Despite this, the Reserve Bank of Australia is unlikely to raise interest rates at its December meeting. Economists anticipate inflation pressures will ease through 2027 as this year's shocks subside.

A number of battery projects linked to China are set to receive UK state subsidies despite growing concerns about foreign interference risks to Europe's energy systems. Ofgem, Great Britain's energy regulator, published a provisional shortlist in June for a new long-duration energy storage scheme. Eleven projects using low-cost lithium-ion batteries, some backed by developers relying on Chinese manufacturers, made the 16-strong shortlist. One developer, Field Energy, sources batteries from Envision Energy, linked to the Chinese Communist Party. A UK government spokesperson said energy security remains a priority, with investments subject to strict national security scrutiny.

Xiamen Airlines has agreed to purchase 50 Boeing 737-8 aircraft, with a combined catalogue price of approximately $7.4 billion. The planes are scheduled for delivery in batches between 2031 and 2034. China Southern, which owns a 55% stake in Xiamen Airlines, stated that the purchase will support the airline's planned relocation to the new Xiamen Xiang'an International Airport and the expansion of its route network. The transaction remains subject to shareholder and relevant Chinese government approvals.

Canadian authorities have identified Michael Joseph Moore as responsible for the 1968 death of 16-year-old Jacqueline Dunleavy. Dunleavy was last seen on the evening of January 9, 1968, leaving her job at a variety store in London, Ontario. She was found dead at a school 90 minutes later, with a head injury and strangulation as the cause of death. Moore, who was 25 at the time, had moved to Canada from Ireland in 1967. Officials say if Moore were alive today, he would have been arrested and charged in connection with Dunleavy's death.

Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin, and Xi Jinping met on November 19, 2026, in Shenzhen, China, for a trilateral summit. The meeting focused on international crises and nuclear disarmament. This trilateral meeting took place on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation, or APEC, summit. The leaders discussed pressing global issues in an effort to find common ground on security and diplomatic matters.

The Crimean Bridge has been attacked by Ukraine for the third time this week, according to a Russian official. Traffic was temporarily suspended following the latest strike on the bridge, which connects Russia to occupied Crimea. The critical structure had already sustained severe damage earlier in the week, with part of the bridge collapsing into the water. Aksyonov confirmed the strike in the early hours of Thursday via Telegram, stating that an assessment of the damage was underway. The bridge supports a four-lane roadway and a railroad and is the only link between mainland Russia and occupied Crimea. On Tuesday, Ukraine launched a major attack using missiles and drones, destroying the steel arch of the 19-kilometre-long road section. This attack occurred just hours before a scheduled meeting between President Putin and his US and Chinese counterparts at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit.

Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi received a subdued welcome on her first trip to China as premier. Upon arrival, she was met on the tarmac by a retired Chinese diplomat rather than a serving official. In the leaders' group photo, Takaichi was positioned in the back row, off to the side and several places away from Chinese President Xi Jinping. This reception signals Beijing's lack of enthusiasm to repair ties with Tokyo. Relations between the two countries deteriorated sharply after Takaichi stated in November 2025 that a Taiwan Strait crisis could threaten Japan's survival and justify a self-defence response. Takaichi's visit is the first by a Japanese prime minister to China since Shinzo Abe's trip to Chengdu in December 2019. No formal meeting between Takaichi and Xi Jinping has been publicly scheduled during her visit.

France has raised its avian flu risk level from moderate to high across the entire metropolitan territory as of November 19, 2026. This decision follows the confirmation of several outbreaks in poultry farms and increased infections among migratory wild birds. The highest risk level prompts additional surveillance and prevention measures to prevent the virus spreading to domestic poultry. Recent outbreaks have been detected in backyard flocks in Gironde and Seine-Maritime, as well as in the Landes department. Since autumn 2023, vaccination against avian flu has been mandatory for farms with more than 250 ducks. Earlier this year, in June 2026, the risk level had been lowered to negligible following the 2025-2026 winter epizootic.

Grand Theft Auto VI has launched to rave reviews following 13 years of anticipation. The game began dropping on consoles worldwide from midnight on November 19, 2026. Developed by New York-based Rockstar Games, the production cost is estimated to have exceeded $1 billion. Pre-orders surpassed the estimated 15.8 million units by November 17, generating $1.54 billion in revenue. Industry experts suggest Grand Theft Auto VI is likely to be the most lucrative release in gaming history. Critics have praised the game as a 'masterpiece', 'spectacular', and an 'astonishing American epic'.

A Harvard study has found that the electricity used by 403 of the largest data centres in the US over one year produced about as much carbon dioxide as Denmark. Researchers estimated that between May 2024 and April 2025, these data centres generated 25.7 million tonnes of CO2. More than half of the power consumed came from fossil fuels, mainly natural gas and coal. The study highlights concerns that pledges alone may not be sufficient to reduce emissions. This comes as negotiators at COP31 have launched a voluntary pledge urging data centre operators to adopt cleaner energy sources and to disclose their environmental impact.

The ASEAN summit in the Philippines did not finalise the Code of Conduct for the South China Sea, despite Manila's earlier pledge to complete it this year. The Philippine government reported that 80% of the negotiating text has been completed, with talks set to continue under next year's ASEAN chair, Singapore. The Code of Conduct seeks to manage disputes in the South China Sea, where China claims nearly all the area under its "nine-dash line," a claim invalidated by the Permanent Court of Arbitration in 2016, though Beijing rejects this ruling. Recent tensions include accusations of Chinese coast guard vessels ramming Filipino fishing boats near the Spratly Islands. ASEAN secretary general Kao Kim Hourn expressed optimism, stating, "I don't think it's a failure at all," highlighting potential for future resolution.

Eight NATO allied countries took part in a submarine tracking exercise organised by Sweden in the Baltic Sea. The exercise, named Dynamic Merlin 26, lasted ten days and concluded on November 13, 2026. France contributed an Atlantique 2 aircraft, a submarine hunter capable of carrying up to eight torpedoes in its large ventral bay. This aircraft operates at low altitudes, sometimes descending to 30 metres above the sea to deploy acoustic buoys. The exercise also involved three helicopters from Denmark, Germany, and Sweden. The operation took place near Gotland in the Baltic Sea, approximately 300 kilometres from the Russian enclave of Kaliningrad.

Car use in London is expected to rise by 10% between 2023 and 2036, according to recent modelling. Public transport trips are projected to increase by 17%, while journeys by walking and cycling will grow by 6%, though their share of total trips may slightly decline. The rise in car journeys could reduce average speeds by 7%, potentially slowing bus routes and causing overcrowding on public transport, with possible station closures during peak times on the underground. The modelling challenges Sadiq Khan's 2022 pledge to cut car use by 27% by 2030. Khan's office said the figures are not predictions but based on no new policies and exclude recent changes like Oxford Street pedestrianisation.

An international team of scientists, including researchers from the University of Barcelona, has discovered a rare stonefly insect beside the remains of a glacier in Chilean Patagonia. The find was made next to the remnants of a glacier on Navarino Island, in the Tierra del Fuego archipelago, where ice loss is altering environmental conditions. The discovery is detailed in a study published in November 2026 in the journal Ecology. The insect, belonging to the family Gripopterygidae, has not been matched to any known species, suggesting it may be previously undescribed. The research was led by Javier Rendoll-Cárcamo and involved the University of Magallanes and the British Antarctic Survey.
